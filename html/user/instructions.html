Instructions and Requirements

Application Guidelines for All Types of Leave

General Instruction: Application for any type of leaves shall be made on the prescribed Form and accomplished at least in duplicate with the required documentary requirements.

Vacation Leave As needed
Filing Period: 5 days in advance whenever possible
  • Must indicate if leave is within the Philippines or abroad
  • Required for securing travel authority
  • Must complete clearance from money and work accountabilities
Mandatory/Forced Leave 5 days annually
  • Annual five-day vacation leave shall be forfeited if not taken
  • Cancelled leave due to service exigency won't be deducted
  • One day or more VL counts for mandatory leave compliance
Subject to Section 25, Rule XVI of Omnibus Rules Implementing E.O No. 292
Sick Leave As needed
  • Filed immediately upon employee's return
  • If filed in advance or exceeding 5 days, medical certificate required
  • If no medical consultation, affidavit must be executed
Maternity Leave 105 days
  • Proof of pregnancy (ultrasound, doctor's certificate)
  • Notice of allocation of Maternity Leave Credits (CS Form No. 6a)
  • Seconded female employees enjoy full pay in recipient agency
Paternity Leave 7 days
  • Birth certificate
  • Medical certificate
  • Marriage contract
Special Privilege Leave 3 days
Filing Period: At least 1 week prior (except emergencies)
  • Must indicate if within Philippines or abroad
  • Required for travel authority
  • Must complete money and work accountabilities clearance
Solo Parent Leave 7 days
Filing Period: 5 days in advance whenever possible
  • Updated Solo Parent Identification Card
Study Leave Up to 6 months
  • Must meet agency's internal requirements
  • Contract between agency head and employee
VAWC Leave 10 days
  • Barangay Protection Order (BPO)
  • Temporary/Permanent Protection Order (TPO/PPO)
  • Certification from Punong Barangay/Kagawad or Prosecutor
  • Police report and medical certificate (discretionary)
Rehabilitation Leave Up to 6 months
Filing Period: Within 1 week from accident
  • Letter request with relevant reports
  • Police report (if applicable)
  • Medical certificate on nature of injuries
  • Written concurrence of government physician
Special Leave Benefits for Women Up to 2 months
Filing Period: 5 days prior to surgery
  • Medical certificate from proper authorities
  • Clinical summary of gynecological disorder
  • Duration of surgery and perioperative period
  • Estimated recuperation period
Special Emergency (Calamity) Leave Up to 5 days
  • Maximum 5 days straight or staggered within 30 days
  • Once a year privilege only
  • Head of office verification required
  • Validation of residence in calamity area
Monetization of Leave Credits
  • Letter request stating valid and justifiable reasons
  • Applies to 50% or more of accumulated leave credits
Terminal Leave
  • Proof of resignation, retirement, or separation
Adoption Leave
  • Authenticated copy of Pre-Adoptive Placement Authority
  • Issued by Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD)
  • Authenticated copy of Pre-Adoptive Placement Authority
  • Issued by Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD)

Important Notice

For leave of absence for thirty (30) calendar days or more and terminal leave: Application shall be accompanied by a clearance from money, property and work-related accountabilities (pursuant to CSC Memorandum Circular No. 2, s. 1985).
